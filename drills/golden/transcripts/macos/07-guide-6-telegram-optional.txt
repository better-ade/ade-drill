Step 6 of 6: Telegram (optional)
Optional: a Telegram bot of your own lets you reach your main agent. A yes asks
for the bot's token (hidden), then goes one step at a time; no skips it.
Do you want to reach your main agent from Telegram? [y/N] y
In Telegram, open @BotFather and send /newbot, then give your bot a name.
BotFather replies with the bot's token. Paste it here; it is not shown, and it
goes only to the hub's key store.

Your turn: in Telegram, send your bot any message. It replies with a pairing

Your turn: in the same Claude Code window, type:
